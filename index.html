Race N' Chase'

My 1st group project

About

This project was completed in around 3 months with multiple breaks inbetween. You are a bank robber and must drive away from the police (basic AI) to your safe house. I'm particularly proud of the fact we managed to add (basic and very simple) voice acting for the robber so the you know the context.

Process

Art/Visuals: I stuck to doing all the porgramming for the game as well as doing the UI and start/end scenes, I had to do this as both my other teammates were 3D artists.

Sound/Music: One of my teammates used BandLab to create the background music. For sound effects, I found most of them online and then editing them with Audacity.

Code: I watched many tutorials online to get the basic mechanics and then used the Unity API for most of the rest, learning new things along the way.
